# Admission Document Filler

Fill in and print Nigerian university admission documents — enter your details once, preview the formatted document, then print or save as PDF. No installation, no sign-up, and nothing is uploaded anywhere; everything runs in your own browser.

**Live site:** https://courbyte.github.io/admission-docs/

## What it does

Pick a document, fill in the form on the left, and watch it appear correctly formatted on the right — ready to download and print.

Supported documents:
- **Acceptance Letter** — student to Registrar
- **Guarantor's / Sponsor's Letter** — guarantor to Registrar
- **Affidavit of Good Conduct** — sworn at the Magistrate Court
- **Affidavit of Non-Membership of Secret Cult** — sworn at the Magistrate Court

## Features

- Live preview that updates as you type, laid out and spaced like the original printed documents
- Missing details show up as clear placeholders (e.g. `[STUDENT FULL NAME]`) so nothing gets printed blank by accident
- Address fields automatically wrap into short, evenly aligned lines — however you type them (commas or line breaks)
- One-click **Download document to print**, which opens straight into your browser's print dialog — use "Save as PDF" as the destination if you want a PDF instead of a paper printout
- Works on both desktop and mobile
- A caution banner reminding you to double-check every detail (name, registration number, course, dates) before printing — especially important for the affidavits, which are signed/sworn documents
- Your in-progress details are remembered in your own browser between visits (nothing is sent to a server)

## How to use

1. Open the [live site](https://courbyte.github.io/admission-docs/)
2. Choose a document from the tabs at the top of the form
3. Fill in your details — the preview on the right updates as you go
4. Check the preview carefully against the caution note
5. Click **Download document to print**, then print or save it as a PDF

## Tech

Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no build step, no dependencies, no framework. A single self-contained file.
